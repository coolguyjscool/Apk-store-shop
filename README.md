ITS HERE FINALLY                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            Yes apk store is here but before that let get over some things: # 📦 sniper’s APK Store Archive

Welcome to **sniper’s APK Store Archive**, a curated collection of open‑source, privacy‑respecting Android app‑store clients.  
All APKs are original, unmodified, and preserved exactly as released by their developers.

This project exists to make it easier to explore and understand the Android app‑store ecosystem without relying on centralized services.

---

# 🎯 Purpose

- Provide a safe, organized archive of app‑store clients  
- Preserve original APKs with their signatures intact  
- Offer a single folder for easy downloading and comparison  
- Document credits, sources, and project links  
- Avoid legal issues by not modifying or decompiling anything  

This is an educational and archival project.
